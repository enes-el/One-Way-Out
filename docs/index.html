Welcome to One Way Out!

One Way Out is a point-and-click adventure game that assists you in mathematical problem-solving! Trying to pass your upcoming exam, you come to the conclusion that there is only one way out. Sneaking into the professor's house, you must find the pieces of the upcoming exam sheet in order to get an A. Unfortunately, these pieces are hidden throughout the house, and you must use your problem-solving skills to find them. Good Luck!
